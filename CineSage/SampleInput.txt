**1. Interstellar (2014)**

Interstellar (2014) is a science fiction, adventure, and drama film directed by Christopher Nolan, featuring an IMDb rating of 8.7/10. Starring Matthew McConaughey, Anne Hathaway, Jessica Chastain, and Michael Caine, the movie follows Cooper, a former pilot who joins a team of astronauts on a mission through a mysterious wormhole to find a habitable planet as Earth faces environmental collapse. During their journey, they encounter black holes, time dilation, and extraordinary cosmic phenomena that challenge their understanding of space and time. With a runtime of 169 minutes, the film beautifully explores themes of love, sacrifice, family, human survival, and the mysteries of the universe.

**2. Inception (2010)**

Inception (2010) is a science fiction, action, and thriller film directed by Christopher Nolan, featuring an IMDb rating of 8.8/10. Starring Leonardo DiCaprio, Joseph Gordon-Levitt, Elliot Page, Tom Hardy, and Ken Watanabe, the movie revolves around Dom Cobb, a skilled thief who specializes in entering people's dreams to steal valuable secrets from their subconscious minds. He receives an opportunity to regain his lost life by completing an almost impossible mission of planting an idea inside someone's mind rather than stealing one. As Cobb and his team navigate multiple layers of dreams, they encounter unexpected dangers that blur the boundaries between dreams and reality. With a runtime of 148 minutes, the film explores themes of memory, guilt, imagination, and perception through a gripping storyline and spectacular visual effects.

**3. The Shawshank Redemption (1994)**

The Shawshank Redemption (1994) is a drama and crime film directed by Frank Darabont, featuring an IMDb rating of 9.3/10. Starring Tim Robbins, Morgan Freeman, Bob Gunton, and William Sadler, the movie tells the story of Andy Dufresne, a banker sentenced to life imprisonment for the murder of his wife and her lover, despite claiming his innocence. Inside Shawshank State Penitentiary, Andy faces injustice, cruelty, and years of hardship but gradually builds a meaningful friendship with fellow prisoner Ellis Boyd Redding, known as Red. Through intelligence, patience, and determination, Andy finds ways to survive the harsh prison environment while maintaining his dignity. With a runtime of 142 minutes, the film highlights themes of friendship, freedom, resilience, and the power of hope even in the darkest circumstances.
